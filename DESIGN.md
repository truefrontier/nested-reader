# Nested design system

## Scene

A founder sits at a daylight desk, reading an unfamiliar architecture proposal before approving the build. The screen should feel like a clear, quiet work surface with enough room to keep a question beside its source.

## Direction

The physical reference is a tabbed technical field guide: careful labels, spacious pages, and violet marginalia. The website pairs assertive geometric typography with a large, legible product specimen. The app uses a restrained light palette; the website gives pale violet substantial surface area. No decorative photography or generated raster mockups: the working reader itself is the product imagery.

## Typography

Manrope for the brand and interface, chosen for its rounded, open geometric forms and distinctive numerals after reviewing the Google Fonts specimen. Georgia for long reading passages, giving dense technical work a softer reading rhythm. Brand headlines have tight tracking and fluid sizing; app controls use a compact fixed scale.

## Colors

Use OKLCH tokens: paper near-white with a violet tint; soft lavender sidebar; deep ink; muted plum-gray labels; rich iris for actions and selected passages; warm ochre for unresolved claims. Status always has a text label.

## Layout and components

Desktop reader: 236px library rail, flexible document, 370px contextual inquiry pane. Thin dividers, rounded selected rows, 8–12px controls. No decorative cards around prose. A navigable tree provides the book map. Mobile uses a document-first single pane with explicit Library and Questions toggles. Marketing has an oversized left-aligned promise, large working-product specimen, a connected explanation diagram, narrative feature sections, practical FAQ, and a direct open-reader action.

## Interaction

Select a passage to attach a question. Common actions remain available without selection for keyboard users. Changes have explicit save/cancel and revision history. Async work shows actual stages and can be canceled. Feedback uses inline errors and restrained status messages. Motion is 160–220ms opacity/transform and respects reduced motion.
