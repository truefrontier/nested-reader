# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Founders directing coding agents, developers reviewing designs, and product leads examining AI-generated research. They already have a document and are responsible for acting on it.

## Product Purpose

Turn generated work into understanding the reader can explain, challenge, and use. Open a document, question a passage, follow a connected explanation, and decide what to do. The marketing surface uses the brand register; the reader is the primary product.

## Brand Personality

Calm, inquisitive, precise. A well-made reading instrument. Warm enough to invite questions, rigorous enough to leave uncertainty visible.

## Anti-references

Not a generic chatbot, an infinite feed, a Notion clone, or a noisy dashboard. No fabricated research, proof claims, testimonials, subscription availability, or launch status.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the original passage in sight.
2. Distinguish source material, generated explanations, evidence, and the reader's conclusions.
3. Make depth optional and relationships navigable.
4. Keep the reader's work editable and portable as Markdown.
5. Show what needs another look when a source changes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Implementation decision under the user's instruction to proceed autonomously: target WCAG AA contrast, semantic controls, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced motion, adjustable reading size, and responsive single-pane reading on narrow screens.
