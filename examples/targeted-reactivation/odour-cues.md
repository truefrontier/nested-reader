# Odour cues

Smells were the first cues tried. People learned card positions while a rose scent filled the room, and the scent was released again during slow-wave sleep. Recall improved the next day, and the hippocampus was more active during the scented sleep.

An odour has one advantage over a sound: it does not wake the sleeper. Its disadvantage is that it cannot be tied to one item, so it reactivates everything learned in its presence rather than a single memory.
