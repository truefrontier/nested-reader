# Failure cases worth testing

## A worker disappears

If the process stops after claiming a job, the lease must expire and make the job eligible for another attempt. The lease needs a heartbeat or a duration longer than normal processing.

## A result is saved twice

A unique constraint on the job identifier can prevent two result rows. It does not by itself prevent two calls to a paid external service. That boundary needs its own idempotency design.

## A provider stays unavailable

Retry with bounded exponential backoff and jitter. After a limited number of attempts, mark the job as failed and give the user a deliberate retry action. Keep enough diagnostic information for an operator to investigate without logging the recording itself.

## A new version ships mid-job

A worker should record the processing version on the job. Decide whether retries continue with that version or deliberately migrate. Do not silently mix incompatible intermediate artifacts.

## Test plan

Kill a worker before and after each external side effect. Verify that every job either reaches a terminal state or becomes available for recovery. Check charges independently from database result counts.

These are proposed tests. They have not been run; this book is an illustrative architecture review.
