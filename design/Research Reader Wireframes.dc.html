3Refine mode: scope, instruction, and how a revision reads

Learning grows the session (everything so far); Refine rewrites what's there. ⌘R turns the highlight popover into a Refine popover; ⌘R with nothing selected opens it on the active pane. Scope is the verb you press: selection, document, or corpus (every document in this session). A refinement never overwrites the .md until you accept; until then the page shows the revision in a readable diff. Three diff treatments below to pick from.

1Wireframes: the reading flow, highlight actions, sessions

Assumptions: no sidebar, no toolbar; the window is the page. A Learning Session is the unit of context (a set of md pages grown from one source). Highlighting text is the only "creation" gesture; it offers exactly three verbs: Lookup (inline card), Open (split beside), Later (inserts a link in the source, loads in background). Tabs are shown as a trail, not a tab bar. Read left to right as one story.
